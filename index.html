What is this and how do I use it?

Pack Like a Local suggests what to wear at your destination based on what locals actually wear.

  1. Enter your destination, month, trip length, gender and age group.
  2. Read what locals wear and click + Add on the pieces you want (or Add all).
  3. Add your own items such as toiletries, shoes and documents.
  4. Tick items as you pack them. Your list is saved in this browser.
